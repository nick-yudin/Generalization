## 2022 Papers (Foundational Year)

### January 2022

1. **Grokking: Generalization Beyond Overfitting on Small Algorithmic Datasets**
   Power et al.
   arXiv:2201.02177
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02177)
   *The seminal paper that introduced the term "grokking" - shows neural networks can improve generalization from random chance to perfect performance well after overfitting.*

### May 2022

2. **Towards Understanding Grokking: An Effective Theory of Representation Learning**
   Liu et al.
   arXiv:2205.10343
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10343)
   *Identifies four learning phases: comprehension, grokking, memorization, and confusion. First to show grokking on MNIST.*

### October 2022

3. **Omnigrok: Grokking Beyond Algorithmic Data**
   Liu et al.
   arXiv:2210.01117
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01117](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01117)
   *Extends grokking to images, language, and molecules. Introduces the "LU mechanism" explaining training/test loss dynamics.*

## 2023 Papers (Theoretical Foundations)

### January 2023

4. **Progress Measures for Grokking via Mechanistic Interpretability**
   Nanda et al. (ICLR 2023)
   arXiv:2301.05217
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05217)
   *Reverse engineers transformer algorithms using discrete Fourier transforms. Identifies three phases: memorization, circuit formation, cleanup.*

5. **Grokking Modular Arithmetic**
   Gromov
   arXiv:2301.02679
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02679)
   *Analytic expressions for weights solving modular arithmetic. Shows grokking can occur without explicit regularization.*

### March 2023

6. **Unifying Grokking and Double Descent**
   Davies et al.
   arXiv:2303.06173
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06173](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06173)
   *Proposes unified framework for grokking and double descent. First demonstration of model-wise grokking.*

### April 2023

7. **Towards Automated Circuit Discovery for Mechanistic Interpretability**
   Conmy et al.
   arXiv:2304.14997
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14997](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14997)
   *ACDC algorithm for automated circuit discovery. Rediscovered circuits in GPT-2 Small.*

### May 2023

8. **Grokking of Hierarchical Structure in Vanilla Transformers**
   Murty, Sharma, Andreas, Manning
   arXiv:2305.18741
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18741](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18741)
   *Shows transformers can learn hierarchical generalization after extremely long training. Introduces "structural grokking".*

### September 2023

9. **Explaining Grokking Through Circuit Efficiency**
   Varma et al.
   arXiv:2309.02390
   [https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02390](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02390)
   *Proposes grokking occurs when generalizing solutions are slower but more efficient. Demonstrates "ungrokking" and "semi-grokking".*

### October 2023

10. **Grokking as the Transition from Lazy to Rich Training Dynamics** (ICLR 2024)
    arXiv:2310.06110
    [https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06110](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06110)
    *Proposes grokking arises from transition from lazy to rich feature learning regime.*

11. **Grokking as a First Order Phase Transition in Two Layer Networks**
    arXiv:2310.03789
    [https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03789)
    *Provides analytical predictions on feature learning. Maps grokking to phase transition theory.*

12. **Grokking Beyond Neural Networks: An Empirical Exploration with Model Complexity**
    arXiv:2310.17247
    [https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17247)
    *Shows grokking in Gaussian processes, GP regression, linear regression, and Bayesian neural networks.*

13. **Bridging Lottery Ticket and Grokking: Understanding Grokking from Inner Structure of Networks**
    arXiv:2310.19470
    [https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19470](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19470)
    *Introduces "grokked tickets" from lottery ticket hypothesis. Shows pruning can transfer memorizing to generalizing networks.*

### November 2023

14. **Understanding Grokking Through a Robustness Viewpoint**
    arXiv:2311.06597
    [https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06597)
    *Shows L2 weight norm is sufficient condition for grokking. Proposes perturbation-based acceleration methods.*

15. **Dichotomy of Early and Late Phase Implicit Biases Can Provably Induce Grokking** (ICLR 2024)
    Lyu et al.
    arXiv:2311.18817
    [https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18817)
    *Proves early phase kernel predictor traps followed by sharp transition to min-norm/max-margin predictors.*
